# Business Findings & Recommendations

## Purpose

This document explains the type of business decisions the analysis is designed to support. The included dataset is synthetic, so the emphasis is on demonstrating an end-to-end analytical process rather than claiming real company performance.

## 1. Revenue vs Profitability

Revenue alone does not identify the best-performing products. A product can generate strong sales while delivering weak margins because of cost or discounting.

### Decision Impact

- Track revenue and profit together.
- Flag products with strong sales but weak margin.
- Review pricing and discount strategy before increasing promotional spend.

## 2. Repeat Customers

Customers with multiple purchases are strategically important because repeat behavior can increase lifetime value without requiring the same acquisition effort as a new customer.

### Decision Impact

- Identify high-value repeat customers.
- Build retention campaigns around customer segments with strong lifetime revenue.
- Measure repeat purchase rate over time as a core KPI.

## 3. Customer Cohorts

Cohort analysis shows whether customers acquired in different months continue purchasing after their initial order.

### Decision Impact

- Compare retention across acquisition periods.
- Detect cohorts with weak repeat behavior.
- Measure whether retention initiatives improve future cohorts.

## 4. Product & Category Mix

A category can contribute significant revenue while containing products with very different profitability profiles.

### Decision Impact

- Drill from category to product level before making inventory decisions.
- Prioritize products that combine demand with healthy margins.
- Reduce dependence on low-margin sales volume.

## 5. Discounting

Discounts can stimulate demand but reduce contribution margin. The dashboard therefore includes discount-to-profit analysis rather than evaluating sales volume alone.

### Decision Impact

- Identify products where discounts are associated with weak profitability.
- Test whether higher discounts actually produce enough incremental volume to justify margin loss.

## Recommended Executive Actions

1. Create a retention list of high-value repeat customers for targeted campaigns.
2. Add profit margin thresholds to product performance reviews.
3. Review low-margin, high-revenue products before expanding promotions.
4. Monitor cohort retention monthly to measure customer quality, not just acquisition volume.
5. Use Power BI drilldowns to connect executive KPIs with transaction-level drivers.
